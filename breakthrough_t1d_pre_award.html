Breakthrough T1D: Pre-award Instructions & FAQs

This guide outlines the essential steps for researchers and administrators using RMS360, the grant management system for Breakthrough T1D (formerly JDRF).

Getting Started with RMS360

  • Browser Compatibility: For optimal functionality, use Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox. Avoid using Safari or Edge if possible.
  • Registration: New users must click the orange REGISTER button on the RMS360 login page.
  • Save Often: Always click "Save Draft" frequently to prevent data loss, especially in the Budget sections.

Key Roles in RMS360

Understanding who does what is critical for a successful submission.

PI Principal Investigator (Researcher)

Writes the science. Can complete most sections of the application but cannot formally submit the final proposal to Breakthrough T1D (only to their institution).

RO Research Officer (University Admin)

Represents the institution. Responsible for reviewing the PI's proposal, approving the budget, and formally submitting the application to Breakthrough T1D.

FO Financial Officer

Responsible for financial reporting and expenditure reports post-award. They must be added to the grant during the application phase.

The Application Workflow

  1. LOI (Letter of Intent): The PI submits a brief LOI.
  2. Invitation: Successful LOIs are invited to submit a full proposal.
  3. Drafting: The PI writes the proposal and uploads attachments (biosketches, etc.).
  4. Validation: The PI must click "Validate" to check for errors.
  5. Institutional Submission: The PI submits the proposal to their Research Officer (RO).
  6. Final Submission: The RO reviews and submits the proposal to Breakthrough T1D.

Frequently Asked Questions

Application Content

Q: How do I add my RO and FO to a proposal? Go to the Contacts tab. Select your RO and FO from the dropdown menus. If they are not listed, use the "Click here to create an RO/FO" link to invite them.
Q: Should I include my Biosketch in the "Additional Attachments" tab? No. The PI must upload their Biosketch to their User Profile first (My Profile > Edit). Then, in the application Contacts tab, check the box that says "My Biosketch is up to date." It will auto-append.
Q: What about Co-PIs and Key Personnel Biosketches? Since Key Personnel may not have RMS360 profiles, you should upload their biosketches in the "Additional Attachments" tab or append them to the Research Plan.

Submission Errors

Q: I prematurely submitted my proposal to the RO. What do I do? Contact your Research Officer (RO). They can "Reject" the proposal in the system, which returns it to your queue for editing.
