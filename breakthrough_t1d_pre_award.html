Pre-Award Instructions

Guide for RMS360 Grant Management System

← Back to Global Impact

This guide outlines the essential steps for researchers and administrators using RMS360, the grant management system for Breakthrough T1D (formerly JDRF).

Getting Started with RMS360

  • Browser Compatibility: For optimal functionality, use Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox.
  • Registration: New users must click the orange REGISTER button on the RMS360 login page.
  • Save Often: Always click "Save Draft" frequently to prevent data loss, especially in the Budget sections.

Key User Roles

Understanding the distinction between user roles is critical for successful submission.

PI Principal Investigator
The lead researcher who writes the science. They can complete the application but cannot formally submit it to Breakthrough T1D; they must submit it to their Research Officer first.
RO Research Officer (University Admin)
Represents the institution. They review the proposal, approve the budget, and hold the authority to formally submit the application to Breakthrough T1D.
FO Financial Officer
Responsible for post-award financial reporting. They must be identified and linked to the grant during the application phase.

The Application Workflow

  1. LOI (Letter of Intent): The PI submits a brief LOI.
  2. Invitation: Successful LOIs are invited to submit a full proposal.
  3. Validation: Before submitting, the PI must click "Validate" to check for missing fields.
  4. Institutional Submission: The PI submits the proposal to their Research Officer (RO).
  5. Final Submission: The RO reviews and submits the proposal to Breakthrough T1D.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I add my RO and FO to a proposal? Go to the Contacts tab within the application. Select your RO and FO from the dropdown menus. If they are not listed, use the "Click here to create an RO/FO" link to invite them via email.
Q: Should I upload my Biosketch in the "Attachments" tab? No. The PI must upload their Biosketch to their User Profile first (My Profile > Edit). Then, in the application's Contacts tab, check the box that says "My Biosketch is up to date." It will auto-append to the PDF.
Q: I submitted my proposal to the RO prematurely. How do I get it back? Contact your Research Officer (RO). They can "Reject" the proposal in the system, which returns it to your "My Tasks" queue for editing.
