Pre-Award Instructions

Official Guide for RMS360 Grant Management System

← Back to Global Impact

This guide outlines the essential steps for researchers and administrators using RMS360, the grant management system for Breakthrough T1D (formerly JDRF).

1. Getting Started

  • Browser Requirement: For optimal functionality, you must use Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox.
  • Registration: New users must click the orange REGISTER button on the login page. If your organization does not appear in the dropdown menu during registration, you must submit a "new organization registration" form.
  • User Profile: Before beginning an application, ensure you update the mandatory fields in the Profile section (accessed via your initials in the upper right corner).
  • Data Safety: Click "Save Draft" frequently to prevent data loss, especially when working in the Budget sections.

2. Key User Roles

Understanding the distinction between user roles is critical for successful submission.

PI Principal Investigator
The lead researcher. They can complete most sections of the application but cannot formally submit the proposal to Breakthrough T1D. They must submit it to their Research Officer first.
RO Research Officer (University Admin)
Represents the institution. They review the proposal, approve the budget, and hold the sole authority to formally submit the application to Breakthrough T1D.
FO Financial Officer
Responsible for post-award financial reporting. They complete the expense column in expenditure reports and certify their accuracy.
RD Research Delegate
Designated by the PI to assist with submissions. They can edit most of the proposal (Scientific Progress Reports, etc.) but cannot submit any items.
TTO Technology Transfer Office
Designated by the institution to report all Intellectual Property to Breakthrough T1D.

3. Biosketch Guidelines

RMS360 handles biosketches differently depending on the role. Incorrect uploads are a common cause of errors.

Role Action Required
Principal Investigator (PI) Upload to your User Profile first. Then, in the application Contacts tab, check the box "My Biosketch is up to date" to auto-append it.
Co-PI Must be registered in RMS360. They must upload their biosketch to their own Profile. It will auto-append once they are linked to the grant.
Key Personnel Do not need RMS360 accounts. You must manually upload their biosketches as a PDF in the "Additional Attachments" tab.

4. Submission Workflow

Step 1: Letter of Intent (LOI)
The PI completes the LOI and clicks the Submit LOI button.

Step 2: Full Proposal (Two-Stage Submission)
1. PI Action: The PI completes the proposal and clicks "Validate." Once valid, the PI clicks "Submit to RO".
2. RO Action: The RO reviews the budget in the "Pending Budget Approval" tab. Once approved, the RO clicks "Submit to JDRF".

5. Troubleshooting & FAQs

Q: How do I add my RO and FO to a proposal? Go to the Contacts tab. Select your RO and FO from the dropdown menus. If they are not listed, use the "Click here to create an RO/FO" link to invite them. The RO must be approved by your institution before they appear in the list.
Q: I submitted my proposal to the RO prematurely. How do I get it back? You cannot retrieve it yourself. You must contact your Research Officer (RO). They have a "Send back to PI" button in their approval tab which returns the proposal to your queue for editing.
Q: What if I forget my password? Use the "Forgot your username" or "Forgot your password" links on the main login page.
