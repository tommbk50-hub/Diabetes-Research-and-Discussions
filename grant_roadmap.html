Grant Lifecycle Roadmap

Visualizing the RMS360 Workflow

← Back to Global Impact

This roadmap illustrates the critical stages of the Breakthrough T1D grant process, highlighting the handover points between the Principal Investigator (PI) and the Research Officer (RO).

1

Letter of Intent (LOI)

The process begins here. The researcher submits a brief concept note. If successful, they are invited to submit a full proposal.

Action: PI
2

Proposal Creation & Validation

The PI writes the full science case and uploads Biosketches. Crucially, they must click "Validate" to check for errors before clicking "Submit to RO".

Action: PI
3

Institutional Approval

The proposal moves to the University Admin (RO). They review the budget and hold the final authority to click "Submit to Breakthrough T1D".

Action: RO
4

Award Activation

Once funded, the grant is not active yet. The PI validates the startup materials, and the RO must enter bank details and approve the activation.

Action: PI Action: RO
5

Post-Award Management

The ongoing lifecycle. The PI submits Scientific Progress Reports, while the Financial Officer (FO) submits Expenditure Reports.

Action: PI + FO
