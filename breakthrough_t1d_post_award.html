Post-Award Instructions

Managing Active Grants in RMS360

← Back to Global Impact

This guide provides technical instructions for managing active awards, financial reporting, and project milestones in RMS360.

1. System Basics

  • Browser: Always use Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox for optimal functionality.
  • Passwords: If you forget your password, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. Your username is your email address.
  • Contact Info: To update your details, click My Profile (upper right) and then the blue Edit button (upper left).

2. Post-Award Roles

PI Principal Investigator
Accesses all reporting materials. Completes activation, renewal, and progress reports. Submits items to the RO.
RO Research Officer
Completes payment details for activations/renewals. Certifies and submits expenditure reports to Breakthrough T1D.
FO Financial Officer
Completes the expense column in expenditure reports and certifies accuracy before submitting to the RO.
TTO Technology Transfer Office
Designated by the institution to report all Intellectual Property.

3. Activations & Renewals

Submitting an Activation

Step 1 (PI): Log in, select "Awaiting Activation", and click "View/Edit".
Step 2 (PI): Complete tabs for Contacts, Support, Ethical Certifications, and Budget (Year 1).
Step 3 (PI): Click "Validate and Submit to RO".
Step 4 (RO): Log in, select "Pending RO Approval", review the budget, and click "Edit/Submit" to finalize.

Note: For "Budget Currency Exchange Rate", enter "0" for AUD grants or "1" for USD grants.

Renewals

For multi-year grants, the PI must submit an Annual Progress Report and work with the RO to submit a Renewal application.

4. Financial Management

Adding Payment Details

This is an RO task. Go to "My Profile" > "Add Payment Details". Select Domestic or International, save draft, upload the official bank letter, and save.

Expenditure Reports

Step 1 (FO): Open the report from the "Expenditure Reports" tab.
Step 2 (FO): Complete expenses. Provide justification for variances (highlighted in red). Click "Submit to RO".
Step 3 (RO): Review the report in "Pending RO Approval". If correct, click "Submit to Breakthrough T1D".

5. Project Management

Budget Updates

Step 1: The PI must email the Administrator to request a budget update. Once created by the admin, a link appears in the PI's "Activities Due" column.
Step 2: PI completes the update and submits to RO. RO reviews and submits to Breakthrough T1D.

No-Cost Extensions (NCE)

A link appears 90 days prior to the grant end date. The PI must upload a progress report and signed expenditure report, then submit to the RO. The RO gives final approval.

Ethical Documents

The PI must upload approval letters (IRB/IACUC) under the "Active Grants & Renewals" tab. Click the blue Ethical Documents button.

6. Administrative Changes

  • Milestones: Contact the Scientific Program Manager to request changes.
  • Transfers & Transitions: The PI must email the Administrator immediately. This usually requires a "Termination" activity for the old institution and a new application from the new one.
  • Intellectual Property: The TTO logs in, views the grant, and clicks "Create IP" to submit details.
