Post-Award Instructions

Managing Active Grants in RMS360

← Back to Global Impact

This guide provides technical instructions for managing active awards, financial reporting, and project milestones in RMS360.

1. System Basics

    [cite_start]
  • Browser: Always use Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox for optimal functionality[cite: 1830].
  • Passwords: If you forget your password, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. [cite_start]Your username is your email address [cite: 1888-1889].
    • [cite_start]
  • Contact Info: To update your details, click My Profile (upper right) and then the blue Edit button (upper left) [cite: 1908-1910].

2. Post-Award Roles

PI Principal Investigator
Accesses all reporting materials. Completes activation, renewal, and progress reports. [cite_start]Submits items to the RO [cite: 1958-1961].
RO Research Officer
Completes payment details for activations/renewals. [cite_start]Certifies and submits expenditure reports to Breakthrough T1D [cite: 1964-1966].
FO Financial Officer
[cite_start]Completes the expense column in expenditure reports and certifies accuracy before submitting to the RO [cite: 1968-1969].
TTO Technology Transfer Office
[cite_start]Designated by the institution to report all Intellectual Property[cite: 1976].

3. Activations & Renewals

Submitting an Activation

[cite_start]Step 1 (PI): Log in, select "Awaiting Activation", and click "View/Edit"[cite: 1997].
[cite_start]Step 2 (PI): Complete tabs for Contacts, Support, Ethical Certifications, and Budget (Year 1) [cite: 2016-2017].
[cite_start]Step 3 (PI): Click "Validate and Submit to RO"[cite: 2048].
[cite_start]Step 4 (RO): Log in, select "Pending RO Approval", review the budget, and click "Edit/Submit" to finalize [cite: 2050-2051].
[cite_start]

Note: For "Budget Currency Exchange Rate", enter "0" for AUD grants or "1" for USD grants[cite: 2067].

Renewals

[cite_start]

For multi-year grants, the PI must submit an Annual Progress Report and work with the RO to submit a Renewal application [cite: 2069-2071].

4. Financial Management

Adding Payment Details

This is an RO task. Go to "My Profile" > "Add Payment Details". [cite_start]Select Domestic or International, save draft, upload the official bank letter, and save [cite: 2115-2133].

Expenditure Reports

[cite_start]Step 1 (FO): Open the report from the "Expenditure Reports" tab[cite: 2412].
Step 2 (FO): Complete expenses. Provide justification for variances (highlighted in red). [cite_start]Click "Submit to RO" [cite: 2443-2445].
Step 3 (RO): Review the report in "Pending RO Approval". [cite_start]If correct, click "Submit to Breakthrough T1D" [cite: 2500-2531].

5. Project Management

Budget Updates

Step 1: The PI must email the Administrator to request a budget update. [cite_start]Once created by the admin, a link appears in the PI's "Activities Due" column [cite: 2555-2557].
Step 2: PI completes the update and submits to RO. [cite_start]RO reviews and submits to Breakthrough T1D [cite: 2588-2635].

No-Cost Extensions (NCE)

A link appears 90 days prior to the grant end date. The PI must upload a progress report and signed expenditure report, then submit to the RO. [cite_start]The RO gives final approval [cite: 2677-2757].

Ethical Documents

The PI must upload approval letters (IRB/IACUC) under the "Active Grants & Renewals" tab. [cite_start]Click the blue Ethical Documents button [cite: 2177-2178].

6. Administrative Changes

    [cite_start]
  • Milestones: Contact the Scientific Program Manager to request changes[cite: 2242].
  • Transfers & Transitions: The PI must email the Administrator immediately. [cite_start]This usually requires a "Termination" activity for the old institution and a new application from the new one [cite: 2761-2772].
    • [cite_start]
  • Intellectual Property: The TTO logs in, views the grant, and clicks "Create IP" to submit details[cite: 2897].
